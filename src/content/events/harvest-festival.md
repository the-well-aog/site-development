Our Harvest Festival is our gift to Pocola: a safe, joyful evening for families. Expect games, candy, hot dogs, and a lot of smiles.

Everything is free. Come meet our church family, and let us serve you.

**Want to help?** We need volunteers for games, food, and greeting. Contact the church office to sign up.
