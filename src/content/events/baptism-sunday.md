“We were buried with Him through baptism into death, that just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life” (Romans 6:4).

If you have put your trust in Jesus and have not yet been baptized in water, this is your day. Every candidate meets with a pastor beforehand to talk about what baptism means. Bring a change of clothes and a towel, and invite your family and friends to celebrate with you.

Contact the church to sign up.
