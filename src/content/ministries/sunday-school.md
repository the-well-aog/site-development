“All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness” (2 Timothy 3:16). Sunday School is where we slow down and study it together.

## What to expect

Classes meet at 10:00 AM, an hour before our 11:00 AM worship service. Each class opens the Bible, talks honestly about what it says, and prays together. There is room for questions, whether you have followed Jesus for decades or are just starting to read.

## A class for every age

Children learn Bible stories and memory verses in classes made for them, and adults study books of the Bible and practical topics side by side. Ask a greeter and they will walk you to the right room.

## Why it matters

Sunday School is one of the best ways to get to know people at The Well. A smaller class is where names are learned, prayers are shared, and friendships begin.
