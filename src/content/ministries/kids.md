Jesus said, “Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of God” (Mark 10:14). We take that seriously. Every Sunday your kids will worship, hear a Bible story told for their age, and learn to pray and hide God’s Word in their hearts.

## What to expect

- **Nursery (birth to age 3):** a calm, clean room with caring adults, so parents can worship with peace of mind.
- **Preschool and elementary:** worship songs, a Bible lesson, a memory verse, small-group activities, and time to pray together.
- **Safety first:** families check in at the kids area, and every child is returned only to the adult who checked them in. Kids volunteers are screened before they serve.

## Your first Sunday

Arrive about ten minutes early and a greeter will walk you to the kids area. We will ask for your child’s name and age, any allergies, and a phone number. Then you are free to enjoy the service. If your child needs you, we will let you know right away.

## Faith at home

You are your child’s most important pastor. We will send home the Bible verse and a simple question from each lesson so you can keep the conversation going during the week.
