The first believers “continued stedfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers” (Acts 2:42). That is still the pattern. On Sundays we gather as one church. In groups, we grow together.

## Through the week

Alongside Tuesday prayer and our Wednesday service, smaller groups meet in homes and at the church for Bible study, prayer, and fellowship. Some study a book of the Bible, some follow the Sunday message, and some focus on a season of life, such as men, women, couples, or young adults.

## Joining is simple

You do not need to know your Bible well or have it all together. Come as you are. Reach out and we will help you find a group that fits your schedule and your season.
