The first believers “continued steadfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and in prayers” (Acts 2:42). That is still the pattern. On Sundays we gather as one church. In groups, we grow together.

## Wednesday night

Adults gather on Wednesday nights for prayer and Bible study while students meet in their own space. It is a midweek refill: open the Word, pray for one another, and seek God together.

## Groups through the week

Smaller groups meet in homes and at the church for Bible study, prayer, and fellowship. Some study a book of the Bible, some follow the Sunday message, and some focus on a season of life, such as men, women, couples, or young adults.

## Joining is simple

You do not need to know your Bible well or have it all together. Come as you are. Reach out and we will help you find a group that fits your schedule and your season.
