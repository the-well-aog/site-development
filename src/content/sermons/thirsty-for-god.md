“As the deer pants for the water brooks, so pants my soul for You, O God” (Psalm 42:1). The psalmist was not writing from a mountaintop. He was discouraged, far from the house of God, and surrounded by voices asking, “Where is your God?”

## Big idea

Spiritual thirst is not a sign that God has left you. It is an invitation to come back to Him.

## Notes

1. **Name the dryness honestly.** The psalmist talks to his own soul: “Why are you cast down, O my soul?” (v. 5). Faith does not pretend.
2. **Remember what God has done.** He recalls leading the worship procession to the house of God (v. 4). Memory fuels hope.
3. **Put your hope in God, not in your feelings.** “Hope in God, for I shall yet praise Him” (v. 5). Feelings follow faith.

## Reflect

- Where does your soul feel dry right now?
- What has God done in your life that you need to remember this week?
