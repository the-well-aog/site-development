Jesus “needed to go through Samaria” (John 4:4). Most travelers avoided it. Jesus went straight to a well at noon to meet a woman everyone else avoided.

## Big idea

Jesus knows your whole story and still offers you living water.

## Notes

1. **Jesus crosses every barrier.** Race, gender, religion, and reputation all said no. Jesus said yes.
2. **He offers what no well can give.** “Whoever drinks of the water that I shall give him will never thirst” (v. 14).
3. **He tells the truth in love.** Jesus named her past without shaming her, then revealed Himself as the Messiah (vv. 16–26).
4. **Encounter leads to witness.** She left her water jar and told her town, “Come, see a Man who told me all things that I ever did” (v. 29). Many believed.

## Reflect

- What “water jar” might Jesus be asking you to leave behind?
- Who in your life needs to hear what Jesus has done for you?
