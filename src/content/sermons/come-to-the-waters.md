“Ho! Everyone who thirsts, come to the waters; and you who have no money, come, buy and eat” (Isaiah 55:1). Isaiah pictures a marketplace where the merchant gives everything away.

## Big idea

Grace is free to us because it was costly to Jesus.

## Notes

1. **The invitation is for everyone.** “Everyone who thirsts” leaves no one out.
2. **We spend ourselves on what does not satisfy.** “Why do you spend money for what is not bread?” (v. 2). Success, approval, and pleasure never fill the soul.
3. **Seek the Lord while He may be found.** God promises abundant pardon to the one who turns to Him (vv. 6–7). Today is the day to respond.

## Reflect

- What have you been “buying” to satisfy your thirst?
- What would it look like to receive God’s grace as a gift instead of trying to earn it?
